# Football data glossary

## League

A named football competition whose identity remains stable across seasons.

## Season

One edition of a League, identified by its starting calendar year.

## Stage

An ordered portion of a Season, such as Apertura, Clausura, a group phase, or a knockout phase.

## Round

An ordered matchday or knockout step within a Stage. Its number and display name are not identity.

## Fixture

A scheduled or played match between a home Team and an away Team in a Round.

## Standing Table

A ranked set of Teams calculated for a declared scope, such as a Stage, group, annual aggregate, or relegation average.

## Provider Alias

A private association between an external provider identifier and this system's canonical identity.
